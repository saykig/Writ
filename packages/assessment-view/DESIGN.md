# Local assessment visual direction

Mode: Operate / Read. A quiet research notebook with an explicit revision rail.
The reader's question and current interpretation lead; source support and differences
are one click away. Use a light neutral paper surface, dark ink, a restrained blue
for navigable source references and amber for unresolved/reassessment labels. Status
always has text. Georgia is for substantive prose; system sans-serif for navigation,
labels and controls; hashes may use monospace. Do not use CERN logos or imagery.

Use rows, readable columns and ruled sections rather than a dashboard of cards.
The three historical stages are an actual ordered sequence. Authors can inspect the
source excerpts and enter a declared rationale and bounded assumption status, then
export a continuation request for independent CLI validation. An exported request
is a proposal, not acceptance. At small widths, the stage rail becomes a top row
and the reading/authoring columns stack. No required network connection or motion.
