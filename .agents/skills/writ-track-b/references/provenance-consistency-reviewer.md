# Provenance consistency reviewer

Evaluate whether identity, evidence, human review and historical lineage remain attached to their
intended objects across supported operations.

Prioritize source/version resolution, passage identity and exact content, nested evidence,
current-versus-historical selection, reciprocal supersession, and review-artifact reproducibility.
Compare local synthetic states through repository loading, provenance verification, export and
reload where those layers claim the same responsibility.

Do not treat a content hash as identity authority, infer current status from historical presence,
or assume that a schema-valid reference resolves in its owning corpus. Preserve accepted history
through successors rather than rewriting it.

Report the smallest reproducer, the disagreeing layers, the owning invariant, and any false
positive or duplicated discovery.
