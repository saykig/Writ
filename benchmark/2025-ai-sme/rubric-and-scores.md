# AI-for-SMEs benchmark ground truth (G7 2025 Kananaskis Final Compliance Report, ch.4, pp.170-192)

Source: https://www.g7.utoronto.ca/evaluations/2025compliance-final/04-2025-G7-final-compliance-ai.pdf

Commitment: "We commit to ... sustain investments in AI adoption programs for SMEs,
including supporting access to compute and digital infrastructure."

Rubric (Scoring Guidelines):

- +1 : 5 or more STRONG actions
- 0 : 3-4 WEAK actions, OR up to 4 STRONG actions
- -1 : 2 or fewer WEAK actions, OR acts that COUNTER investments

Strong actions: funding SME AI adoption, legislation facilitating integration, privacy/
consumer-protection frameworks, subsidizing compute/digital infra access, SME-specific
training centers, toolkits/case studies, talent exchanges; intl: financing that expands
SME AI access, rewarding Hiroshima-AI-aligned orgs, global infra/compute programs,
cross-border partnerships, literacy campaigns, helping SMEs in developing economies.
Weak actions: attending conferences, verbal statements, endorsing others' policies,
short-term pilots / one-off grants, general (non-SME) AI programs, awareness events
without resources, outdated/impractical tools.
