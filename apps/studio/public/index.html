Auditable compliance · G7 commitments

“up to four strong actions.”

Five words decide a country’s score. Read as one-to-four, a state with zero strong actions and five weak ones matches no rule. Read inclusively, the same state matches two. The 2025 G7 AI-for-SMEs rubric never says which.

Covenant compiles a scoring methodology to a typed intermediate form and evaluates it with a four-valued engine over a frozen, reviewed body of evidence. The ambiguity has to be resolved on purpose, in writing, before any score is assigned. What comes out is a receipt: a result, a proof tree, and the content hashes that reproduce it.

  1. 01

    Compile & analyze

    The methodology becomes a typed canonical IR. A static analyzer enumerates the score program’s input space and reports every gap and overlap before any evidence exists, each with the exact witness that breaks it.

  2. 02

    Evaluate

    A deterministic, four-valued engine reads a frozen evidence snapshot, classifies the eligible actions, and selects a single score branch. No wall-clock, no network, no randomness.

  3. 03

    Reproduce

    The result is a content-addressed receipt: proof tree, matched rule, qualifying actions, and five bound hashes. Identical inputs give a byte-identical receipt.
