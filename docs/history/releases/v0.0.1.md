## What Writ was trying to do

The repository began as Covenant, an executable compliance and policy-evaluation system. It tried to
turn source material, structured rubrics, claims, actions, and methodology rules into deterministic,
inspectable evaluations. During the period the language was renamed Writ and the project expanded
through G7, G20, EU–US, Gap Matrix, API, database, and web experiments.

## What existed at this checkpoint

The tree contained a Bun monorepo with canonical IR, a four-valued truth model, deterministic
evaluator and analyzer packages, a language toolchain, implementation-independent conformance data,
content-addressed provenance, a source registry, PostgreSQL evidence storage, APIs, ingestion code,
and an in-repository web application. The G7 AI-for-SMEs benchmark, G20 Rio material, and a reviewed
EU–US pilot were all active parts of a product still framed largely around evaluation and compliance.

## Major decisions

- ADRs 0001–0004 were Accepted: canonical IR preceded syntax; truth retained `unknown` and
  `contested`; evaluation was deterministic; and provenance used content identity.
- ADRs 0005–0008 were Accepted: source admission was operational policy, PostgreSQL preceded any
  graph database, models proposed while humans reviewed and code applied rules, and negative claims
  required an explicit search protocol.
- ADRs 0009–0012 were Accepted: actions had stable identity, discrepancies were released rather than
  silently repaired, diagnostics used a shared catalog, and Bun governed the TypeScript toolchain.

All twelve ADRs stood as Accepted at the checkpoint. Later architecture retired several of their
concrete applications without rewriting their historical status.

## Major implementation/research work

This period predates the repository's pull-request sequence. Important commits bootstrapped the
monorepo, evaluator and analyzer, language, evidence ledger, G7/G20 corpora, web surfaces, Gap Matrix,
and EU–US pilot. Commit `4accd1b23dc5f652167f3f64940aa2dabfb6b6a2` renamed the Covenant language
to Writ. The later PR #1 audit identified this target as the exact clean pre-reset state.

## What worked

Deterministic execution, explicit truth states, structured diagnostics, and traceable source
references were demonstrable. The G7 pilot recorded 87 actions for eight members and reproduced all
eight source-reported ratings. It also exposed interpretation-sensitive outcomes for Japan and the
United States instead of hiding them. The Gap Matrix experiment produced one numeric result and kept
another dimension pending when support was insufficient.

## What did not survive

The compliance-first product boundary, evaluator/analyzer runtime, Cepheus and Gap Matrix product
surfaces, repository web application, duplicate reference implementation, and PostgreSQL persistence
were later retired. G7/G20 and EU–US ceased to be active product corpora. The architecture attempted
too many methods, applications, and domains before establishing a sufficiently narrow evidence
boundary.

## What survived

Canonical identity, deterministic transformation, four-valued truth, explicit uncertainty,
content-addressed provenance, typed diagnostics, human review boundaries, and the practice of
preserving discrepancies remain visible in current Writ.

## Known limitations at the checkpoint

The experiments had no user validation and only bounded evidence of generality. The reset audit
found stale generated Next declarations affecting root type checking and a missing local `fitz`
dependency that blocked the complete Python/G20 path. Source-grounded records, questions,
presentation, and compliance judgments were not yet separated cleanly. The root manifest had 11
listed paths absent and 51 digest mismatches; its recorded hash identifies the historical file but
does not certify the target inventory.

## What changed next

The repository was audited and reset around source-grounded political knowledge. Compliance stopped
being the governing product definition, Cepheus and Gap Matrix were removed, and corpus/schema
authority became explicit. That transition was an empirical response to this checkpoint rather than
a predetermined roadmap.

## Current retrospective

This is the record of Writ's broad first attempt. Most visible product architecture did not survive,
but the work established several durable correctness instincts: stable identity, explicit unknowns,
determinism, provenance, and inspectable disagreement.
