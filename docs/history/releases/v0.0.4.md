## What Writ was trying to do

Writ narrowed its foundation to evidence-bound records that existed independently of questions,
comparisons, analyses, and presentation. NIST became the active institutional proving ground for
testing whether source, passage, review, identity, and correction could be made inspectable.

## What existed at this checkpoint

The compliance execution runtime and obsolete corpus consumers were gone. NIST records and review
history had stronger source/version checks, explicit human-approved corrections, and immutable
supersession. Current governance was consolidated, and the long-running HTTP application surfaces
were retired. Repository-native corpora, verification, bundles, ingestion, and the still-retained
database/storage layer remained.

## Major decisions

- ADR 0021 was Accepted: Writ reset to evidence-bound records, with questions and human reasoning
  outside the active system boundary at this checkpoint.
- ADR 0022 was Accepted: the compliance evaluator, analyzer, benchmark, and obsolete language
  execution surface were retired.
- ADR 0023 was Accepted: NIST became the sole active development corpus; specialized ADR-0019
  workflow/rule machinery was retired while generic links and verification remained.
- ADR 0024 was Accepted: long-running HTTP applications were retired while database/storage
  infrastructure was deliberately retained for a later decision.

ADRs 0001–0020 remained present with their historical statuses. ADRs 0019 and 0020 remained
Accepted even though ADR 0023 retired their specialized workflow/rule implementation.

## What worked

NIST demonstrated evidence-linked correction without rewriting accepted history. Mechanical checks
could fail closed on missing sources, source-version mismatch, quotation hashes, ambiguous
references, broken supersession, and invalid endpoints while leaving semantic classification to
human review. Retiring compliance and HTTP surfaces did not alter the retained corpus content or
bundle sections.

## What did not survive

The claim that NIST should define Writ's whole roadmap was later narrowed; it remains a proving
ground for one surface. The universal “questions are external” boundary was later replaced by two
separate layers, one of which preserves explicit decision questions. The retained Postgres/Neon
persistence chain was retired in PR #45.

## What survived

The independence of corpora from downstream analysis, immutable supersession, explicit distinction
between mechanical verification and human semantic judgment, NIST as an active grounding proving
ground, and a repository-native verification surface remain current.

## Verification and evidence at the time

PR #29 reported 76 records, ten links, 52 judgments, unchanged bundle section hashes, 53 Python
tests, and 527 verified manifest entries. It also proved that the different outer bundle hash was
caused only by embedded commit identity. The target's root `MANIFEST.sha256` has SHA-256
`53129fcc107b837f236195146f78d70aab83902d22883fbf06eac8e40fe55617` and verifies when
the target tree is archived.

## Known limitations at the checkpoint

Retired NIST HTML whole-document hashes were not independently reproducible offline. Schema and
mechanical provenance could not decide whether a classification was substantively adequate. Writ
did not yet prove document-to-passage extraction generally, authenticate reviewers, or represent
derived mathematical decision work.

## What changed next

European Commission transfer corrections exposed generic provenance gaps. Writ then documented its
canonical JSON identity, hardened exact contract dispatch and native provenance, and extracted the
portable mechanical kernel.

## Current retrospective

This checkpoint is the cleanest transition from a many-surface repository to an evidence-bound
knowledge foundation. The earlier NIST tag preserves the named reference moment; this version adds
the final application retirement required to make the architectural boundary true in code.
