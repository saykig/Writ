## What Writ was trying to do

Writ tested whether its provenance discipline could extend into one executable mathematical
decision case without confusing checked mathematics, empirical applicability, human disposition,
or authority to act. Governance then recast the project around two deliberately separate surfaces:
source-grounded knowledge and bounded derived decision work.

## What existed at this checkpoint

The `derived_decision_case` package bound exact case, revision, source, passage, model, query,
candidate, certificate, and checker inputs to a pinned Decision Lab implementation. A verified
source-only snapshot isolated execution from unverified repository files. Independent checking,
applicability, declared human disposition, reuse, and authority remained distinct. The synthetic
failure-choice example demonstrated immutable revisions and portable checking.

The roadmap and product definition described the two-surface model and Bellman's intended role.
Track B role files, Postgres/Neon persistence, the tracked archive root, and obsolete root layout
were retired. Three snapshot tags preserved the exact EU–US, G7, and G20 historical bodies.

## What worked

The case could be freshly executed and independently checked against exact supplied bytes. Stale
subjects, forged or missing certificates, source-span mismatch, undeclared inputs, and unsupported
semantics failed closed. Reuse assessment separated dependency, mapping, semantic-context, and human
disposition changes. The fixture remained portable after relocation, and reviewed corpora remained
unchanged through the database and root cleanup.

## What did not survive

Track B's role files, the Postgres/Neon chain, long-running application infrastructure, and tracked
archive directories were removed. Their useful history remains in Git, migration documents,
snapshot tags, tests, and invariants. The root fixture path was replaced by an explicit example path
without changing semantic identities.

## What survived

The current architecture retains source-grounded knowledge and bounded derived decision work as
separate, composable layers. Exact mathematical subjects, fresh independent checking, explicit
applicability, human disposition, immutable revision, source provenance, and the absence of implied
authority remain the governing boundary.

## Known limitations at the checkpoint

There was one synthetic case, one mathematical profile, and one external adapter. CPython, its
standard library, the operating system, SciPy, and the pinned checker source remained within the
trusted computing boundary. Writ made no empirical adequacy, sandboxing, causal, strategic,
sequential, or authority-to-act claim. ADRs 0026 and 0027 remained Proposed.
