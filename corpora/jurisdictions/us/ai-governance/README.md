# United States AI-governance corpus

This is an independent, source-grounded corpus of reviewed United States AI-governance claims. It
contains 17 substantive atomic claims imported from 12 accepted parent annotations.

The corpus keeps voluntary guidance, federal-agency duties, procurement controls,
contract-mediated vendor duties, proposed policy, adoption, applicability, and enforcement
distinct. It preserves nine verified source documents, 12 anchored passages, and one unresolved
source-coverage record for the NIST AI RMF Playbook overview.

Old `US-##` identifiers resolve through `migration-map.yaml`; they do not govern active references.
The corpus can be queried without executing the archived comparative methodology.
