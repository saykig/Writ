# European Union AI-governance corpus

This is an independent, source-grounded corpus of reviewed European Union AI-governance claims. It
contains 15 substantive atomic claims imported from 12 accepted parent annotations.

The corpus preserves:

- Article 53 and Article 55 duties as legally distinct claims;
- the corrected Article 55(1)(a)-(d) numbering;
- Article 113 application and transition rules as separate claims;
- legal force, compliance function, adoption, applicability, and enforcement as separate fields;
- 12 explicit `unknown` enforcement values;
- local actor and conduct terminology;
- 10 anchored source passages and two unresolved source-coverage records.

The temporary Article 51 and Article 52 assignments remain excluded and are recorded in
`reviews/reconciliation.yaml`. Old `EU-##` identifiers resolve through `migration-map.yaml`; they
do not govern active references.
